## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative yet forward-looking aesthetic tailored for premier government technology symposiums, digital transformation exhibitions, and international innovation summits. Built around the maritime dynamism and digital ascension of Hải Phòng, the visual style fuses crisp light minimalism with subtle high-tech translucent glass elements and institutional prestige.

The emotional objective is absolute institutional trust paired with cutting-edge velocity: clean, airy, highly legible, and free of visual clutter. The interface uses layered luminous white substrates, razor-sharp technical typography, and deep ocean navy structures accented by electric maritime cyan highlights. Visual motifs evoke precision data infrastructure, maritime digital gateways, and high-level civic governance.

## Colors

The palette operates on high tonal contrast calibrated strictly for light mode environments.

- **Primary (`#0284c7`) & Tertiary (`#0099ff`)**: Vibrant Tech Ocean Cyan/Blue signals dynamic interaction, live presentation streams, active agenda tracks, and prominent callouts. It mirrors maritime connectivity and innovation networks.
- **Secondary (`#0f172a` & `#1e293b`)**: Deep Navy commands structural hierarchy. Used for foundational text, critical badges, high-level structural sidebars, and key stage/hall headers to maintain executive GovTech gravitas.
- **Surface Foundations**: Base canvases use absolute white (`#ffffff`), tinted high-tech mist (`#f8fafc`), and structured utility gray (`#f1f5f9`).
- **Semantic Feedback**:
  - Success: `#059669` (Deep Jade) for approved delegations, active speaker check-ins, and confirmed registration.
  - Warning: `#d97706` (Amber Ochre) for limited session capacity and schedule alerts.
  - Danger: `#dc2626` (Red Crimson) for access restrictions and live technical alerts.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional contrast between technological precision and executive clarity.

- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides architectural weight and subtle mathematical character. Ideal for conference keynote titles, room designations, exhibition pavilion metrics, and thematic track headers. Negative letter-spacing maintains tight visual balance at larger sizes.
- **Body & Labels (`Inter`)**: Delivers unmatched clarity at small-to-medium scales for agenda timelines, delegate directories, whitepaper abstracts, and form controls.
- Numbers, timestamps, hall indices, and booth IDs should always employ tabular figures (`tnum`) to maintain structured alignment across schedule matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system engineered for content-dense schedules, speaker rosters, and live streaming multi-views.

- **Grid Breakpoints**:
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, `1rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Stacks session cards, speaker details, and interactive floor plans vertically.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout, `1.5rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Allows dual-track schedule comparisons and side-by-side exhibitor cards.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with a maximum container boundary of `1440px`, flanked by `3rem` canvas margins. Supports persistent summit track navigators, timeline rails, and real-time exhibitor mapping.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Built upon a strict 4px/8px modular base. Content groupings within cards use `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px), while major panel sections and keynotes divide along `space-xl` (40px) intervals.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines tonal layering with crisp glassmorphism outlines, avoiding heavy dark dropshadows to preserve an immaculate light environment.

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - **Tier 0 (Background)**: Pure `#f8fafc` or `#ffffff`.
  - **Tier 1 (Cards, Modules, Schedule Slots)**: Clean `#ffffff` surfaced above `#f8fafc`, framed with an ultra-fine border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
  - **Tier 2 (Sticky Headers, Floating Controls, Glass Panels)**: High-translucency background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle light-catching edge `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
- **Shadow System**:
  - **Level 1 (Resting Cards)**: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
  - **Level 2 (Hover / Active Session)**: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
  - **Level 3 (Modal / Live Booth Inspection Popover)**: `0 20px 35px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.06)`

## Shapes

The design uses a refined curvature profile (`roundedness: 2` = base 8px, `rounded-lg` = 12px, `rounded-xl` = 16px) that feels modern, technical, and approachable without lapsing into toy-like softness.

- **Component Standard (`12px` / `rounded-lg`)**: Applied to standard interactive cards, input fields, speaker profile avatars, dropdowns, and summit session modules.
- **Structural Containers (`16px` / `rounded-xl`)**: Applied to major modal dialogs, featured keynote banners, digital exhibition stage frames, and map viewports.
- **Pill Exceptions (`9999px`)**: Reserved exclusively for operational status tags, session track badges (e.g., "AI & GovTech", "Smart Port Logistics"), and action chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep ocean navy background (`#0f172a`) or vibrant cyan (`#0284c7`) with crisp white text, 12px border radius, font `label-lg`, and zero harsh black shadow. Hover activates subtle brightness transition and a focused cyan ambient ring `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary (GovTech Outline)**: Crisp `#ffffff` fill with 1px border of `#cbd5e1`, text `#0f172a`. Hover transitions to border `#0284c7` with text `#0284c7`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Transparent fill, `#0369a1` text, used for ancillary navigation like "Download Session Slides" or "View Abstract".

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicators**: Full pill (`9999px`), 12px font size, uppercase or small-caps tracking.
  - "Live Now": Background `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.1)`, text `#0284c7`, paired with a pulsing 6px dot.
  - "Plenary Session": Background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, text `#0f172a`.
- **Filter Chips**: Height 36px, 8px radius, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`. Selected state toggles to `#0f172a` fill with `#ffffff` text.

### Cards & Exhibition Panels
- **Session & Speaker Cards**: Constructed on `#ffffff` with 12px or 16px corner radius, encased by `1px solid #f1f5f9`. Header displays stage time in tabular `Space Grotesk`, speaker affiliation in secondary tone `#64748b`, and an accent vertical bar on the left edge corresponding to thematic track color.
- **Exhibitor Booth Showcase**: Incorporates a top visual asset framed in a 16:9 ratio, subtle glass badge showing booth number (e.g., `BOOTH A-12`), and clean call-to-actions.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs & Search**: 12px roundedness, height 44px, background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, placeholder `#94a3b8`. On focus, border snaps to `#0284c7` with a zero-delay soft glow `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom 18px controls with 4px radius (checkbox) or circular (radio), `#0284c7` checked fill with crisp white checkmark vector.

### Lists & Timelines
- Agenda lists utilize a continuous vertical 2px guide in `#e2e8f0` connecting chronological milestones. Active/upcoming time slots feature a glowing cyan node (`#0284c7`), grounding the live conference experience.
